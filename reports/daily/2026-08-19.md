### 最值得執行的三個藍海方向

#### A. Agency QA Policy Memory

- **Target customer:** Webflow/Framer/Shopify agencies and in-house web teams.
- **Pain:** client-specific rules and accepted/rejected QA findings live in comments and reviewer memory; the same mistakes and false positives recur.
- **MVP:** ingest 100–500 historical review comments → classify defect/rejection/preference/exception → version QA policy → replay against staging → human approval history.
- **Pricing:** US$99–299/month/agency; US$300–1,000 initial policy extraction.
- **Acquisition:** Webflow, Framer, Shopify agency communities; migration from manual QA/review tools.
- **Two-week experiment:** 5 agencies; ≥100 historical comments each; replay 3 recent releases. Pass if each agency gets ≥2 reproducible repeated/missed patterns, false positive <10%, and 2 agencies accept US$199/month or US$500 setup.
- **Risk:** native Superflow/browser-QA features, conflicting client policies, private-site access, false positives.

#### B. AI SDR Qualification & Pipeline Auditor

- **Target customer:** B2B teams already paying for AI SDRs and inbound sales agents.
- **Pain:** “meetings booked” is easy to optimize but may not equal held meetings, qualified opportunities or pipeline revenue.
- **MVP:** transcript + CRM join; score qualification rubric adherence, false-qualified leads, handoff quality, held-meeting rate and pipeline conversion; compare agent versions with holdout cohorts.
- **Pricing:** US$199–499/month/team; setup/audit US$500–2,000.
- **Acquisition:** HubSpot ecosystem, AI-SDR agencies, RevOps communities.
- **Two-week experiment:** 5 teams / ≥500 conversations; find at least 3 material calibration or attribution gaps; obtain 2 paid pilots.
- **Risk:** CRM data quality, privacy, causal attribution, incumbent SDR vendors adding analytics.

#### C. Agent Recipe Compatibility & Trust Badge

- **Target customer:** AI workflow creators, skill authors and agent marketplaces.
- **Pain:** a recipe that works today may silently break after model, API, tool schema, credential scope or runtime changes.
- **MVP:** dependency BOM → 10–30 golden fixtures → scheduled replay → `works / degraded / broken / permission-changed` badge + evidence.
- **Pricing:** US$19–49/month/creator; US$99–299/month team/marketplace.
- **Acquisition:** Taku-like marketplaces, MCP/skill communities, Claude/Codex agent builders.
- **Two-week experiment:** 30 public recipes across five change types; pass if ≥10 reproducible break/degradation cases are found and 3 creators pay US$29/month or one marketplace accepts US$299 pilot.
- **Risk:** native conformance, replay safety, private credentials/data, marketplace concentration.

### 核心判斷

Today's capital signal is that inference economics is valuable enough to support US$21B-scale specialized-system bets, while the product-launch signal is that agents/workflows are becoming reusable, tradable and operationalized units. The Solopreneur opportunity is not to reproduce frontier hardware or broad agent platforms; it is to sell **evidence that a workflow still works, is still qualified, or still matches a customer-specific policy after change**.
