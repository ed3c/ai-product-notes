# Wispr Flow — 2026-08-18

## 繁體中文

Wispr Flow 是跨 app 的 AI voice keyboard / dictation product。Reuters 於 2026-08-17 報導 Wispr 完成 US$280M Series B、估值 US$2B，累計融資 US$361M；公司表示產品已被超過 10,000 家企業使用。同日公司公布 Canto preview，這是第一個 proprietary speech-recognition model，主打 noise、wind、accent 與 music 等困難環境。

### 客群、痛點與商業模式
個人用戶需要比 typing 更快的 input surface；企業更在意跨 application adoption、accuracy、security、admin controls 與 measurable time savings。官方定價為 Flow Basic 免費、Flow Pro US$15/user/month，Flow Enterprise custom。

### WTP evidence
US$280M Series B、US$2B valuation、超過 10,000 enterprises 的公司使用數，以及 US$15/user/month Pro 定價形成強 WTP signal。Canto 的困難環境 accuracy 改善數字是公司自述，不能當成 independent benchmark。

本資料庫評分：WTP 9/10、Funding Scale 9/10、User Traction 9/10、Market Gap Moat 8/10；Best Composite = 8.80。

### 競爭與 Market Gap
競爭包含 native dictation、Whisper derivatives、superwhisper、MacWhisper、Aqua Voice、Otter 等。Generic STT 越來越 commodity；更值得收費的 gap 是 domain adaptation、context-aware rewriting、privacy、cross-app ergonomics 與 hard-condition reliability evidence。

### 技術與營運門檻
需要 low-latency streaming ASR、VAD、punctuation/formatting、custom vocabulary、accent/noise robustness、mobile/desktop input integration、privacy controls、telemetry 與 enterprise admin。若自建 model，還需要 speech-data provenance、evaluation corpus 與 inference optimization。

### Solopreneur MVP
**Voice Input Regression Bench**：針對單一 vertical 建立代表性 noise/accent/domain fixtures，對不同 ASR/provider/model version 做 entity fidelity、latency、cost 與 formatting regression。不要再造 generic dictation UI。

### 兩週驗證
建立 100–200 個可合法使用的代表性 fixtures，比較 3 個 ASR stacks，找 5 個 design partners。成功門檻：至少 3 家發現 production-relevant regression，2 家願付 US$99–299/month。

### 風險與結論
主要風險是 audio privacy、evaluation-data rights、accent fairness、provider-native eval 與 fixture maintenance。機會在 domain-specific reliability evidence，而不是另一個 dictation app。

Wispr Flow is a cross-application AI voice keyboard and dictation product. Reuters reported on 2026-08-17 that Wispr raised a US$280M Series B at a US$2B valuation, bringing total funding to US$361M. The company says Flow is used at more than 10,000 enterprises and also previewed Canto, its first proprietary speech-recognition model.

Official business pricing lists Flow Basic as free, Flow Pro at US$15/user/month, and Enterprise as custom. Internal Best scores are WTP 9/10, Funding Scale 9/10, User Traction 9/10 and Market Gap Moat 8/10, for a Composite of 8.80.

The competitive gap is moving away from generic speech-to-text toward domain vocabulary, cross-app ergonomics, privacy and evidence that quality survives difficult real-world conditions. A Solopreneur MVP should be a **Voice Input Regression Bench** for one vertical, comparing representative fixtures across ASR providers and model versions on entity fidelity, latency, cost and formatting.

Validate with 100–200 representative fixtures, three ASR stacks and five design partners. Require three teams to discover production-relevant regressions and two paid pilots at US$99–299/month. Main risks are audio privacy, data rights, fairness and provider-native evaluation.
