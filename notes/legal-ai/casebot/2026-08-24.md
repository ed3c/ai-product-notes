### 目標客群與核心痛點

目標客群是 law firms、corporate legal、eDiscovery、investigations、privacy 與 regulatory teams。高價值痛點不是「找文件」，而是把大量 matter data 轉成可驗證、可辯護的 professional work product，同時保留 source lineage、matter-level access controls 與 audit trail。

### 商業模式、定價與 WTP

HaystackID 將 CaseBot / Case Insight 放進 managed eDiscovery / Core Intelligence AI capacity programs，採 configured monthly fee rather than simple self-serve seat pricing。2026-08-23 官方文章以 Am Law midlevel associate rates 推估，一份手工 issue-organized fact narrative、cited chronology 或 deposition kit 可達 US$5K–14K+。這是 vendor-provided cost anchor，不是 audited customer savings；但 legal expert labor 的高單價與 HaystackID 已有 managed subscription model，支持 WTP 9/10。

### 競爭與 Market Gap

競爭包含 Harvey、CoCounsel、Spellbook、Luminance、Relativity ecosystem 以及 law-firm internal AI。CaseBot 的差異化是：

- matter data 原地分析；
- source-cited factual output；
- Relativity / eDiscovery workflow proximity；
- Specialized Skill Agents 對應 repeatable legal work products；
- existing access controls / audit trails。

Gap 不在 generic legal chatbot，而在「professional AI output 是否逐 claim 可追溯、chronology 是否一致、引用是否支持結論、版本變更後是否仍 defensible」。

### 技術與營運門檻

- Evidence retrieval / source anchoring
- Claim-to-source span verification
- Entity / chronology consistency
- Privilege / matter-access isolation
- Prompt injection / hostile document handling
- Auditability, retention, purge, export
- Human legal review and professional-liability boundary

### Solopreneur MVP：Evidence-Bound Professional Work Product QA

不要直接提供法律建議。MVP 放在「AI 已產生 work product」之後做 independent QA：

1. 解析 narrative / chronology / memo 中的 factual claims；
2. 每一 claim 必須對應 source span；
3. 檢查 citation entailment、日期/人物/金額 consistency；
4. 找 contradictory evidence / missing support；
5. 輸出 review packet，讓專業人士作最終判斷。

初期選單一 workflow，例如 litigation chronology 或 regulatory investigation memo。Pricing 可測 US$500–2,000 / matter，或 US$199–999/month/team。

### 兩週驗證

- 建立 30–50 個 rights-cleared / synthetic matter fixtures。
- 植入 unsupported claim、wrong citation、timeline conflict、entity mismatch、omitted contradictory evidence。
- 找 3–5 位 legal/eDiscovery professionals review blind outputs。
- 成功門檻：material defect recall ≥90%、false positive <10%，並取得至少 1 個 US$1K paid pilot 或 2 個 US$299/month pilots。

### 主要風險與結論

最大風險是 legal liability、client confidentiality、privilege、data residency，以及「citation exists」不等於「legal conclusion correct」。產品定位必須是 evidence QA / review acceleration，而不是 attorney replacement。CaseBot 的 next-generation update 是高價值訊號，因為它顯示企業 legal AI 正從 conversational retrieval 走向 structured, cited work products；這會讓 independent claim/evidence QA 成為更清楚的下游需求。

## English

### Product and company overview

CaseBot is the legal and investigation matter-intelligence assistant from eDiscovery AI, a HaystackID company. It reached general availability on May 11, 2026. On August 23, 2026, HaystackID announced the next generation of CaseBot with agentic retrieval, Specialized Skill Agents, and structured outputs such as fact narratives, cited chronologies, deposition kits, and record checks.

### Customer, pain, business model, and WTP

The buyer is a law firm, corporate legal team, eDiscovery provider, investigator, privacy team, or regulatory-response team. The expensive problem is producing defensible professional work from large matter records while retaining source lineage and access controls. HaystackID sells CaseBot inside configured managed-capacity programs. Its August 23 article estimates that comparable manual expert deliverables can cost US$5K–14K+ based on Am Law associate-rate assumptions. This is vendor-provided cost evidence, not audited savings, but it supports a high WTP score.

### Competition and market gap

Alternatives include Harvey, CoCounsel, Spellbook, Luminance, Relativity-linked tools, and internal law-firm systems. The useful gap for a small product is downstream verification: claim-to-source support, citation entailment, chronology consistency, contradiction detection, and review receipts.

### Solopreneur MVP

Build an Evidence-Bound Professional Work Product QA layer. It should parse factual claims from an AI-generated chronology or memo, require a source span for each claim, detect unsupported or contradictory evidence, and create a review packet for a qualified professional. Do not present it as legal advice.

### Two-week validation

Create 30–50 synthetic or rights-cleared matter fixtures with planted unsupported claims, incorrect citations, timeline conflicts, and entity mismatches. Have three to five legal/eDiscovery professionals review blind results. Continue only if material-defect recall reaches at least 90%, false positives remain below 10%, and at least one US$1K pilot or two US$299/month pilots are secured.

### Risks and conclusion

Legal liability, privilege, confidentiality, and data residency are the main operational risks. Source citation is necessary but not sufficient for a correct legal conclusion. The strongest opportunity is independent evidence QA, not replacing lawyers.
