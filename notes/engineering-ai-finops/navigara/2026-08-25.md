## 繁體中文

### 產品與公司概況

Navigara 在 2026-08-24 Product Hunt 日榜進入 #3。其 AI ROI 產品將 AI coding spend、engineering capacity 與 roadmap work 連結，試圖回答「花在 Cursor / Claude / coding agents 的錢，實際推進了哪些 roadmap item、多少屬於 maintenance / testing / fixing / off-roadmap work」。這是一個從 AI tool cost monitoring 往 engineering business outcome attribution 移動的訊號。

### 目標客群與痛點

目標 buyer 是已大量使用 AI coding tools 的 CTO、VP Engineering、Engineering Manager、FinOps / DevEx teams。痛點不是「token 花太多」，而是管理者無法可靠回答：

- AI spend 是否真的推進 roadmap？
- 哪些 spend 被 maintenance、test/fix loop、rework 或 off-roadmap work 吃掉？
- 不同 coding agent/model 的成本與 delivered outcome 是否成比例？
- AI adoption 是否只是提高 code volume，卻沒有改善 cycle time / quality？

### 商業模式與定價

Navigara 公開 pricing 提供 14-day trial，Pro 約 **US$30/developer/month**；AI ROI Audit 從約 **US$4,500 one-time** 起，Enterprise 為 custom / on-prem。這是很強的直接 WTP signal：buyer 願意為 engineering AI spend 與 roadmap attribution 付費，而不是只買另一個 coding assistant。

### WTP evidence

- US$30/developer/month recurring pricing。
- US$4,500 起的一次性 audit，顯示 decision report 本身可單獨 monetise。
- 產品展示 roadmap-linked spend、feature/maintenance/test/fix breakdown 與 capacity metrics。
- 官方案例與 ETV/ROI methodology 屬 vendor-defined evidence，不能直接視為獨立 causal proof；真正需要驗證的是「這些 metric 是否會改變 budget / roadmap / tool-selection decision」。

### 競爭與 Market Gap

相鄰市場包含 engineering analytics（LinearB、Jellyfish、DX 等）、AI FinOps、LLM observability、coding-agent analytics 與 vendor usage dashboards。Gap 在於大多數工具停在 seats/tokens/PRs/LOC；真正的 executive decision 需要：

`AI spend → roadmap outcome → cycle time → quality/rework → maintenance burden → business priority`

差異化應建立在 roadmap-level attribution、cross-agent normalization、historical decision receipts，而不是再發明一個 developer score。

### 技術與營運門檻

技術上可由 GitHub/GitLab、Linear/Jira、CI、coding-tool invoices/usage exports 與 deployment/incident data組合，因此適合 Solopreneur；困難在 identity resolution、issue/commit mapping、causality 與避免 Goodhart's Law。

### Solopreneur MVP：AI Coding ROI & Roadmap Ledger

第一版只做 read-only，不提供 employee ranking。

資料流：

`Git/PR/commit → Linear/Jira roadmap item → coding-agent/provider spend → CI/review/rework/deploy → roadmap outcome receipt`

輸出：

1. 每個 roadmap item 的 AI spend range。
2. Feature vs maintenance/test/fix/off-roadmap spend。
3. Rework / rollback / escaped-defect burden。
4. Cross-provider/model cost comparison。
5. Weekly budget/roadmap decision receipt，而不是個人 productivity leaderboard。

建議價格：**US$99–499/team/month**；initial ROI audit 可收 **US$1K–3K**。

### 兩週驗證計畫

- 招募 5 個每月 coding-AI spend ≥ US$5K 或至少 10 名 AI-heavy developers 的 teams。
- 匯入最近 4–8 週 Git + Linear/Jira + coding-tool invoices/usage。
- 手動協助 mapping 20–50 個 roadmap items。
- 找出 off-roadmap spend、rework、maintenance burn 或 provider-cost tradeoff。
- 通過門檻：至少 3 家認為 insight 會改變 tool/budget/roadmap decision，並取得 2 個 **US$199/month** paid pilots 或 1 個 **US$1K audit**。

### 主要風險

- Correlation 不等於 causation。
- Commit/issue mapping 可能錯誤。
- 個人績效評分會導致 Goodhart / team distrust，第一版應明確禁止 employee ranking。
- Source code / roadmap / billing 都是高敏感資料，需要 least-privilege integration 與 strong data deletion controls。
- GitHub、Linear、coding-tool vendors 可能加入原生 spend analytics；moat 必須是 cross-vendor roadmap attribution。

### 結論

AI coding 的下一個付費層不是「更多 autocomplete」，而是 **證明 AI spend 是否真的轉成 roadmap value**。這個問題的 buyer、價格與可量化 ROI 都比 generic developer analytics 更清楚，適合先以 audit-to-SaaS 路徑驗證。

## English

### Product and company

Navigara ranked #3 on the 2026-08-24 Product Hunt board. Its AI ROI product connects AI coding spend and engineering capacity to roadmap work, moving the problem from token/seat monitoring toward outcome attribution.

### Customer and pain

The buyer is a CTO, VP Engineering, Engineering Manager, DevEx, or FinOps team already spending materially on coding agents. They need to know whether AI spend advances roadmap work or is absorbed by maintenance, testing, fixing, rework, and off-roadmap activity.

### Business model and WTP

Public pricing includes a 14-day trial, roughly **US$30/developer/month** for Pro, an AI ROI Audit starting around **US$4,500 one-time**, and custom Enterprise/on-prem terms. This is direct evidence that buyers will pay for the decision layer around coding-AI spend.

### Evidence boundary

Navigara's ETV/ROI methodology and customer examples are vendor-defined and should not be treated as independent causal proof. The real validation target is whether the metrics change budget, roadmap, or provider decisions.

### Solopreneur MVP

**AI Coding ROI & Roadmap Ledger**: read Git/PRs, Linear/Jira roadmap items, coding-provider spend, CI/review/rework/deployment events, and generate a roadmap-level spend/outcome receipt. Avoid employee productivity rankings.

Suggested pricing: **US$99–499/team/month**, with an initial **US$1K–3K** ROI audit.

### Two-week validation

Recruit five AI-heavy software teams, ingest 4–8 weeks of Git + roadmap + AI-tool billing data, map 20–50 roadmap items, and identify actionable off-roadmap spend, rework, maintenance burn, or provider-cost differences. Pass if three teams report a decision-changing insight and two buy US$199/month pilots or one buys a US$1K audit.

### Risks

Causality, identity/mapping errors, Goodhart effects, sensitive code/roadmap/billing data, and native analytics from coding vendors. The durable moat is cross-vendor roadmap attribution plus historical decision receipts.

### Verdict

A strong Solopreneur wedge is the **financial/accountability layer for AI-assisted engineering**, not another coding agent.
