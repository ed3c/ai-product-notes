## Hard laws

- Highest earned closure is the longest unbroken PASS prefix from `SOURCE_ANCHORED` through `PAID_VALIDATED`; it is recomputed, never trusted from prose.
- `SOURCE_DOCUMENT` and `ISSUE_RECORD` cannot close implementation, runtime, user, or paid rungs.
- `CI_RUN` cannot close `IMPLEMENTED`, `LIVE_WORKFLOW_VERIFIED`, `USER_VALIDATED`, or `PAID_VALIDATED`.
- Design/compiler CI cannot be substituted for an owned product implementation suite.
- `MODEL_JUDGMENT` closes no rung.
- `ABSENT`, `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`, `NOT_EXERCISED`, `SKIPPED_BY_POLICY`, and `HUMAN_ADMIT_REQUIRED` remain distinct.
- Upstream `UNKNOWN` claims and unresolved contradictions remain in scope until exact evidence resolves them.
- Issue deltas are proposals with `NO_WRITE_AUTHORITY`; Shadow does not mutate Builder paths or close issues.
- Public snapshots contain no private reasoning and require no prior conversation.
